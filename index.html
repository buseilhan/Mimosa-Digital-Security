Our Movement

Secure Identities. Safe Future. Empowered Society.

Mimosa is a volunteer-driven movement dedicated to protecting digital identities — not as a service, but as a shared responsibility.

Digital safety is no longer just a technical issue. It's a growing equity gap for those with limited access to information, low awareness, or limited resources. Mimosa closes that gap not by selling a product, but by sharing knowledge, mobilizing volunteer experts, and empowering communities.

  • Digital safety is a right, not a privilege.
  • Awareness is the strongest line of defense.
  • Real impact comes from volunteer effort and collaboration.

Our volunteers support individuals and communities through workshops, awareness campaigns, and one-on-one guidance.

Joining Mimosa is not taking on a task — it is becoming part of a movement. We are safer together.

Why We Started

We started Mimosa because we kept seeing the same thing: people who had the least access to information were the ones most exposed to digital harm. Not because they weren't careful — because no one had ever sat down and explained the risks in a way that made sense to them.

So we built a movement, not a product. A group of volunteers — security professionals, educators, everyday people who care — who show up to run workshops, answer questions, and help people understand what's actually at stake with their digital identity.

We don't think digital safety should depend on how much you can pay for it. It should just be something everyone has access to, the same way we'd expect anyone to know how to lock their front door.

If that sounds like something you'd want to be part of — as a volunteer, a partner, or just someone who believes in the same thing — we'd genuinely love to hear from you.
